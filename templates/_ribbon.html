{# The severity ribbon — the console's signature element. A raster of recent events, newest first, one tick per event, tick colour is the event's severity. Ticks are a fixed thickness stacked from the top, so the ribbon reads as a comb: a fat block means a quiet channel, a dense comb means a flood, and the comb's shape shows when the trouble was. The free space under it is itself the signal — nothing has come in. `events` newest-first rows, already capped by the view. `project` the project to link ticks to; omit on the index, where each tick resolves to its own project. The ribbon is aria-hidden and its ticks are tabindex="-1". It repeats what the event list already says, and 48 focusable links with no text would be a trap for anyone navigating by keyboard. Each tick is still a real link for the mouse, and still announces its level and title on hover. #} {% macro ribbon(events, project=None) %}
